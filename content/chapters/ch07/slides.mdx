<Slide title="Two workloads in one server">

<Cols>
<div>

**Prefill**

- Whole prompt in one pass; batch is T tokens.
- Compute-bound. Sets time to first token.
- 8k tokens of 70B: 0.91 s on 16 v5e, 157 ms on 8 Ironwood.

</div>
<div>

**Decode**

- One token per sequence per step.
- Reads every weight and every cache.
- Memory-bound. Sets time per token.

</div>
</Cols>

</Slide>

<Slide title="Decode step time">

$$
T_\text{step} = \frac{B \cdot \text{KV}}{W_\text{hbm}} + \max\left(\frac{2B \cdot \text{params}}{C}, \frac{\text{param bytes}}{W_\text{hbm}}\right)
$$

<Gloss>Attention always reads every cache; the MLP is weights or FLOPs, whichever is longer.</Gloss>

$$
B_\text{crit} \approx \frac{C_\text{math}}{W_\text{hbm}} \cdot \frac{\text{bytes per weight}}{2}
$$

| | bf16 | fp8 weights, bf16 math | fp8 throughout |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ironwood | 313 | 156 | 313 |
| 8i (provisional) | 128 | 64 | 512 |

<Slide title="Sharding decode">

- **No FSDP:** weights over ICI every step, an order-of-magnitude regression.
- **No in-server DP, no sequence parallelism:** use replicas.
- **Model parallelism, far:** ICI binds only when $Y > F/(B\beta_\text{ici})$; $\beta_\text{ici}$ ≈ 8 on v5e, 37 on Ironwood.
- **KV cache:** by heads (at most K ways), then by batch with two AllToAlls per layer.
- **Collectives are latency-bound:** 8-way below 360 kB (v5e), 800 kB (Ironwood).

</Slide>

<Slide title="Disaggregated serving">

- Prefill servers: compute, little memory, batch 1.
- Decode servers: bandwidth and HBM for many caches, heavy model parallelism.
- Cost: shipping KV caches between them.

<Big n="3 : 1">prefill to decode servers for 8k prompts and 512-token answers (book example)</Big>

</Slide>

<Slide title="Quantization and other levers">

- **Weights to 8 bits:** halves the weight read and capacity; B_crit halves if math stays bf16.
- **KV to fp8:** halves the dominant long-context term.
- **Native fp8 math:** Ironwood and TPU 8; v5p and v6e list fp8 at the BF16 rate.
- **Qwix:** intercepts dot_general, einsum and ragged_dot; regex rules per module.
- **Speculative decoding:** k tokens scored for the cost of one in a memory-bound step.
- **MoE:** E/k × the batch, latency-bound AllToAlls.

</Slide>
