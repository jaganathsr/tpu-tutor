<Slide title="Sharding notation">

| Notation | PartitionSpec | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| $A[I_X, J_Y]$ | `P('X', 'Y')` | I over X, J over Y |
| $A[I_{XY}, J]$ | `P(('X','Y'), None)` | I over X and Y together |
| $A[I, J]\{U_X\}$ | `unreduced={'X'}` | partial sums still to add over X |

- One mesh axis per array dimension at most; unmentioned axes replicate.

</Slide>

<Slide title="Four cases for A[I, J] · B[J, K]">

| Case | Sharding | Collective |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | J not sharded on either | none |
| 2 | J sharded on one operand | AllGather it |
| 3 | J sharded on both, same axis | AllReduce, or ReduceScatter at half cost |
| 4 | I and K sharded on the same axis | AllGather one first |

Tensor parallelism's second matmul is case 3. FSDP's weights are case 2.

</Slide>

<Slide title="Ring collective costs">

$$
T_\text{AG} = T_\text{RS} = \frac{V}{W_\text{ici}}, \quad T_\text{AR} = \frac{2V}{W_\text{ici}}, \quad T_\text{A2A} = \frac{V}{4W_\text{ici}}
$$

<Gloss>V per device; independent of ring size. Divide by the number of axes used. Floor: N/2 µs of hop latency.</Gloss>

| Time | 5 ms | 10 ms | 1.25 ms | 1.7 ms |

<Slide title="Collective matmul">

- Split the AllGather into N − 1 collective-permutes; multiply the shard you have while the next one arrives.
- XLA: windowed einsum, above `xla_jf_spmd_threshold_for_windowed_einsum_mib`.
- Book, shard_map by hand: 311 µs → 244 µs, against 224 µs with no communication.
- GPU equivalents: PyTorch async-TP (SymmetricMemory), TE userbuffers, Flux. Same algorithm (Wang et al.), more machinery, because NCCL competes for SMs.

</Slide>

<Slide title="Against NCCL">

- Same ring math: busbw factors $(n-1)/n$ and $2(n-1)/n$.
- H100 node AllReduce: about 370 GB/s only at about 10 GB messages; a 58 MB tensor-parallel AllReduce gets about 150 GB/s.
- TPU v5e AllGather: about 95% of peak at about 10 MB.
- AllGather of bf16[1024, 16384] over 8 devices: H100 node 65 µs; one Ironwood axis 168 µs; three axes about 56 µs.

</Slide>
