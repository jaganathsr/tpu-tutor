<Slide title="No switch in the data path">

- Each chip has direct ICI links to its nearest neighbours: 4 on v5e and v6e, 6 on v4, v5p and Ironwood.
- Messages to distant chips hop through intermediate chips, at about 1 µs per hop.
- Per-chip bandwidth stays constant as the pod grows: about 9,000 chips at full ICI bandwidth.

</Slide>

</Slide>

<Slide title="Topologies">

<Cols>
<div>

- **v5e, v6e:** 16×16 2D torus, 256 chips. Wraps only on 16-long axes.
- **v4, v5p, Ironwood:** 4×4×4 cubes joined by optical switches into a 3D torus. Wraps on every axis of a whole number of cubes.

</div>
<div>

- **Twisted tori:** offset wraparound, +70% bisection on 4×4×8.
- **8i Boardfly:** 4-chip boards, 8-board groups, 36 groups. At most 7 hops across 1,152 chips.

</div>
</Cols>

<Slide title="Optical circuit switches">

<Cols>
<div>

- MEMS mirrors steer light between fibres; reconfigure in milliseconds.
- No packets read, no buffers, no in-network reduction.

</div>
<div>

- Slices of any shape from non-adjacent cubes.
- Route around failed racks: 99.98% availability on v4.
- Under 5% of system cost.

</div>
</Cols>

<Slide title="Latency or bandwidth">

$$
T \approx \text{hops} \cdot 1\,\mu\text{s} + \frac{\text{bytes}}{\text{links} \cdot W_\text{link}}
$$

<Cols>
<div>
<Big n="192 µs">16.8 MB corner to corner on a v5e 4×4: bandwidth-bound</Big>
</div>
<div>
<Big n="16 hops">across an 8×8×16 slice: a small decode collective is latency-bound</Big>
</div>
