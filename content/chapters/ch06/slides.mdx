$$
\alpha = \frac{C}{W_\text{ici}}

<Gloss>FLOPs a chip must do per byte moved on one torus axis.</Gloss>

| Chip | α |
|---|---|
| v5p | 2,550 (book), 2,295 (docs) |
| Ironwood | 11,535 |

<Slide title="Conditions for compute-bound training">

| Scheme | Condition | v5p | Ironwood |
|---|---|---|---|
| DP / FSDP | $B/X > \alpha/M_X$ | 850 (3 axes) | 3,845 |
| TP | $Y < M_Y F / \alpha$ | 11-way | 2.5-way |
| FSDP + TP | $B/N > \alpha^2/(M_X M_Y F)$ | 113 | 2,320 |
| DP over DCN | $B/\text{slice} > C / W_\text{dcn}$ | 73,440 | 185,000 |

For LLaMA-3-70B ($F$ = 28,672), tokens per chip or per slice.

<Slide title="GPU recipe for comparison">

- H100 α: 2,200 in node, 2,475 across nodes. Similar thresholds; different topology.
- NVLink stops at 8 GPUs, so TP stays in the node and PP + DP carry the scale.
- LLaMA-3 405B: 16,384 H100s, TP 8 × PP 16 × DP 128, 16M tokens, 41% MFU.
- DeepSeek-V3: 2,048 H800s, EP 64 × PP 16 × DP 2.
