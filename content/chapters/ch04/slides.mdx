<Slide title="Three numbers every later chapter divides">

- **Parameters:** what HBM must hold and what decode reads every step.
- **FLOPs:** 6 × parameters × tokens to train; 2 × active parameters per token to infer.
- **KV-cache bytes:** $2 L K H$ × bytes per token, per sequence, read every decode step.

</Slide>

<Slide title="Counting">

| Component | Parameters per layer | Training FLOPs per layer |
|---|---|---|
| Gated MLP | $3DF$ | $18BTDF$ |
| Attention projections | $2DH(N+K)$ | $12BTDH(N+K)$ |
| Dot-product attention | none | $12BTSNH$ |
| Vocabulary | $2DV$ total | $6BTDV$ |

LLaMA-3-70B: MLP 56.4B, attention 12.1B, vocab 2.1B. 6.3e24 FLOPs on 15T tokens: 45 days on a v5p pod, 9 on an Ironwood pod at 40% MFU.

<Slide title="Attention: small share, wrong intensity">

<Cols>
<div>

$$
\frac{\text{attn FLOPs}}{\text{matmul FLOPs}} = \frac{T}{8D}
$$

<Gloss>Attention dominates only beyond 65,536 tokens for D = 8,192.</Gloss>

</div>
<div>

$$
\text{AI}_\text{attn} = \frac{TSG}{TG + S}
$$

<Gloss>Prefill: grows with T. Decode: about G ≈ 8, at any batch.</Gloss>

</div>
</Cols>

Decode attention is memory-bound at every batch size: each sequence reads its own cache.

</Slide>

<Slide title="The KV cache sets decode batch">

<Cols>
<div>
<Big n="320 KB">per token, LLaMA-3-70B in bf16</Big>
<Big n="10.7 GB">per 32k-token conversation</Big>
</div>
<div>

- 8 Ironwood chips: 141 GB of weights, about 1.5 TB free.
- About 140 conversations at 32k fit.
- The matmuls want 313 tokens per chip, 2,500 across 8 chips.
- Capacity, not FLOPs, limits the batch.

</div>
</Cols>

</Slide>

<Slide title="MoE: memory follows E, compute follows k">

$$
B > \frac{E}{k} \cdot \beta \cdot \frac{C}{W_\text{hbm}}
$$

<Gloss>Each expert sees only its share of tokens, so the batch must be E/k times larger.</Gloss>

| | E/k | Compute-bound batch |
|---|---|---|
| DeepSeek-V3 shape, v5e, int8 weights | 32 | 3,840 |
| Same, Ironwood, fp8 weights, bf16 math | 32 | about 5,000 |
| Same, Ironwood, fp8 math too | 32 | about 20,000 |

</Slide>

<Slide title="Training memory is not the constraint">

- Adam: 10 bytes per parameter. 700 GB for 70B.
- LLaMA-3-70B at a 4M-token batch: 11.2 TB with activations. Fits on 54 Ironwood chips.
- On 54 chips it would take years. Over a pod it is 1.3 GB per chip.

</Slide>

<Slide title="Shape on TPU">

- Pad D, F, H and V to multiples of 256 (v6e, Ironwood). LLaMA-3 already is.
- Head dim 128 fills half a 256-deep MXU per head; 64 fills a quarter.
- K = 8 KV heads: split the cache by heads at most 8 ways, then by batch.
- Ragged MoE batches fight static shapes: capacity padding, ragged_dot, megablox, SparseCore.

</Slide>
