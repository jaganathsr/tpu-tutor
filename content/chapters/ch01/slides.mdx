<Slide title="Two times bound every kernel">

$$
T_\text{math} = \frac{\text{FLOPs}}{C}, \qquad T_\text{comms} = \frac{\text{bytes}}{W}
$$

<Gloss>Arithmetic over peak FLOP/s; bytes over the bandwidth of the link that carries them. Overlapped: the max. Not overlapped: the sum, at most 2× worse.</Gloss>

- Compute-bound when FLOPs per byte exceeds the ridge point, $C / W_\text{hbm}$.
- On TPUs the compiler prefetches HBM into VMEM, so overlap is the default.

</Slide>

<Slide title="Precision moves the ridge">

$$
B_\text{crit} \approx \frac{C_\text{math}}{W_\text{hbm}} \cdot \frac{\text{bytes per weight}}{2}
$$

- int8/fp8 weights, bf16 math: $B_\text{crit}$ halves. v5e 240 to 120; Ironwood example 329 to 164.
- fp8 math too: peak doubles, ridge 625, $B_\text{crit}$ back to 329. Step time still halves: 64 to 32 µs.
- TPU 8i fp8 ridge about 1,024 <Provisional />. So 8i bought bandwidth and 384 MB of SRAM instead of FLOPs.

</Slide>

<Slide title="What the HBM roofline leaves out">

- **VPU work:** its own roofline, critical intensity about 3 on v5p. Cheap only when fused.
- **Latency:** about 1 µs per ICI hop. Under about 45 kB per hop on v5e, latency dominates.
- **Achievable peak:** the book's estimate is 80–85% on H100/B200 and about 95% on TPU; the measurements are not published.
