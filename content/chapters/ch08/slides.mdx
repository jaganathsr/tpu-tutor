<Slide title="torchax">

- `torchax.Tensor` is a `torch.Tensor` holding a `jax.Array`.
- `__torch_dispatch__` maps each ATen op to `jnp` / `lax`; `jax.jit` traces it to StableHLO.
- The model must be a pure function: `model_fn(weights, kv_caches, inputs) -> (kv_caches, ...)`.
- The "sandwich": PyTorch model code inside; JAX sampler, KV cache and RPA kernel around it.

</Slide>

<Slide title="Continuous batching with static shapes">

- Flatten every query token in the step into one array; metadata carries per-sequence structure.
- Pad token and sequence counts to buckets; compile them all before serving.
- Ragged Paged Attention handles true lengths inside the kernel: fused KV write, three sub-kernels (decode, prefill, mixed).
- 86% bandwidth in decode, 73% FLOPs in prefill (Llama-3-8B, Ironwood).

</Slide>

<Slide title="KV cache layout">

$$
(\text{blocks}, \text{block size}, \lceil 2K/p \rceil, p, \text{head dim}), \qquad p = \frac{32}{\text{bits}}
$$

<Gloss>K and V interleaved, packed 2 (bf16) or 4 (8-bit) per 32-bit sublane: the (8,128)(2,1) and (4,1) tilings.</Gloss>

Mesh: `('data', 'model')` by default; heads, KV heads, MLP and experts on model. Also `expert`, `attn_dp`, `dcp`, `pcp`.

</Slide>

<Slide title="Case study: LLaMA-3-70B on v5e">

- int8 KV: 164 kB per token. Batch 32 at 8k: 113 GB, fits a 4×2.
- 4×2: 17 ms per step, about 235 tokens/s per chip. 4×4: 8.6 ms, same per chip.
- bf16 on 4×8 at batch 64: HBM 18 µs, ICI 12 µs, math 5 µs. HBM-bound.
- Bigger slices fit disproportionately more KV: about 1.6× tokens/s per chip from doubling.

</Slide>
